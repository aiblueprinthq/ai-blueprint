# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AI Blueprint welcomes practical, respectful participation. Contributors and
maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks
- give specific, constructive feedback
- respect different experience levels and points of view
- keep public conversations free of harassment, threats, and discrimination
- avoid publishing private information, credentials, or confidential material

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or demeaning comments,
deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption, and
publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

For an urgent conduct concern, use GitHub's private reporting tools when they
are available or the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
Do not open a public issue that exposes a target or repeats harmful content.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
